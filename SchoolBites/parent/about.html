Our Purpose

Making School Meals Easier for Parents.

Planning healthy school meals every day can be difficult. SchoolBite brings meal planning, child preferences, subscriptions and delivery into one simple experience for parents.

Why SchoolBite

Good Food. Happier Kids. Peace of Mind for Parents.

We believe children deserve nutritious meals they enjoy, while parents deserve a simple and reliable way to manage them.

Nutritious Meals

Flexible Subscriptions

Everything Parents Need in One Place.

Add Your Child — Create profiles with school, class, dietary preferences and allergy information.

Explore Weekly Menus — View upcoming meals and understand the nutrition details before choosing.

Choose a Meal Plan — Select one of the available subscription plans that suits your needs.

A Simpler Parent Experience.

Manage Your Subscription — View, pause, resume or manage your subscription according to the available options.

Track Orders & Payments — View your order history, payment details and subscription transactions.

Track Delivery — Check upcoming deliveries and delivery status from the Parent Dashboard.
